# Challenge Letter Response Templates

## Acceptance Responses (by tone/prestige level)

### High Prestige Accept - Cordial
```
Dear Sir,

The Secretary of {recipient_club} acknowledges with great pleasure your kind letter of the {sent_date}.

We are delighted to accept your proposal for a match of football between our respective clubs. The date of {proposed_date} suits our fixtures admirably, and we look forward with keen anticipation to what promises to be a most sporting contest.

Your terms regarding {venue_description} and {stakes_description} are most agreeable to us, and we shall be honoured to meet your gentlemen on the field of play under {rules_type}.

We remain confident that the match shall be contested in the finest spirit of sportsmanship that has become the hallmark of Sheffield football.

Your obedient servant,
Secretary, {recipient_club_short}
```

### Accept - Formal
```
Dear Sir,

The {recipient_club} Football Club begs to acknowledge receipt of your communication dated {sent_date}.

After due consideration by our Committee, we are pleased to inform you that your proposal for a match has been accepted. The proposed date of {proposed_date} is suitable, and we agree to the terms as outlined in your letter.

Arrangements shall be made accordingly, and we anticipate a keenly contested match under {rules_type}.

We have the honour to be, Sir,
Your most obedient servants,
The Committee of {recipient_club}
```

### Accept - Enthusiastic
```
Dear Sir,

What capital news! Your challenge letter arrived this morning and has been received with tremendous enthusiasm by all our members.

We accept with the greatest pleasure! The {proposed_date} could not suit us better, and we are already in training for what we confidently expect shall prove a splendid exhibition of the football code.

{venue_description} is most agreeable, as are your proposed stakes. We shall meet your challenge under {rules_type} with determination and sporting spirit.

May the best eleven triumph!

Your obedient servant,
Secretary, {recipient_club_short}
```

## Declined Responses

### Decline - Schedule Conflict
```
Dear Sir,

We acknowledge with thanks your kind invitation to a match on {proposed_date}.

It is with sincere regret that we must inform you that we have already engaged to meet {other_club} on that date, and our Committee feels it would be improper to break such an engagement.

Might we suggest the {alternative_date} as a more suitable occasion? Should this prove agreeable, we would be most happy to accept your sporting challenge.

We remain, Sir,
Your obedient servants,
{recipient_club}
```

### Decline - Too Soon
```
Dear Sir,

We are in receipt of your letter proposing a match on {proposed_date}.

While we are most grateful for your invitation, we must respectfully decline as several of our principal players are presently indisposed, and we fear we could not field our strongest eleven at such short notice.

We would be honoured to arrange a match at a later date, perhaps in {alternative_month}, when we might better hope to provide your gentlemen with worthy opposition.

Your obedient servant,
Secretary, {recipient_club_short}
```

### Decline - Prestige Mismatch (Polite)
```
Dear Sir,

We thank you most kindly for your sporting letter of {sent_date}.

While we are sensible of the honour you do us in proposing a match, our Committee feels that at present our club's commitments to matches with clubs of similar standing must take precedence in our fixture list.

We wish your club every success in the coming season and trust you shall find suitable opponents for your gentlemen to test their mettle against.

We remain, Sir,
Your obedient servants,
{recipient_club}
```

## News Story Templates

### Challenge Sent (Player's Club)
```
CHALLENGE LETTER DISPATCHED

A letter of challenge was sent by post this morning to the Secretary of {recipient_club}, proposing a match to be played on {proposed_date} {venue_description}.

The terms proposed include play under {rules_type} for {match_duration} minutes, {stakes_description}.

Our Secretary has expressed confidence that the challenge shall be well-received, and we eagerly await their response, which should arrive within the coming days.
```

### Challenge Received (Player's Club)
```
CORRESPONDENCE ARRIVED THIS MORNING

A letter arrived by the morning post from {sender_club}, challenging our club to a match of football.

The Secretary of {sender_club} proposes that we meet on {proposed_date} {venue_description}, to play under {rules_type} for {match_duration} minutes.

[Button: Read the Letter]

The Committee shall review this sporting proposal and formulate an appropriate response.
```

### Response Received - Accepted
```
{recipient_club} ACCEPTS CHALLENGE

Splendid news! A letter has arrived from {recipient_club} accepting our challenge.

Their Secretary writes that the proposed date of {proposed_date} suits their fixtures admirably, and they look forward with keen anticipation to the contest.

The match has been duly entered into our fixture list. Training shall commence forthwith to ensure we are in proper condition for this important engagement.
```

### Response Received - Declined
```
{recipient_club} DECLINES MATCH

We are in receipt of a letter from {recipient_club} declining our proposed match on {proposed_date}.

Their Secretary cites {reason} and expresses regret at being unable to accept our sporting challenge at this time.

The Committee shall consider whether to propose an alternative date or to seek other worthy opponents for our gentlemen.
```
